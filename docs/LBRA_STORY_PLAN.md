# Little Big Racing Adventure: the story (plan)

The user, 2026-11-03: "Plan out a story line for our racing adventure. The game needs to progress through all the tracks with story line
parts in-between. The track order that they're already listed in is the order we're to encounter them in-game. We should avoid having to
race the same track twice. Let's add in some new quests, we don't need to worry about the original quests."

## The idea in one paragraph

The Esmers land on Citadel Island and, to say thank you for clearing the storm, open **the Two Worlds Cup**: one race on every island of
Twinsun and Zeelich. Whoever wins the whole Cup gets one wish from the Esmers. Twinsen enters. But a masked driver in a black car is
following the Cup. He never races, but he's always at the finish line, and slicks of oil and broken bridges turn up wherever he's been.
Island by island Twinsen finds out who he is: FunFrock, back from LBA1, the same FunFrock Twinsen chased in his dream on the first night.
FunFrock wants the wish so he can bring back the Dark Monk's rule. The Cup ends on Celebration Island, round the risen statue, where the
dream comes true.

## Rules for every chapter

- **One track, raced once.** Each chapter ends with its race. A loss means an immediate rematch on the same track ("Again?"), never a later
  return. Once a race is won, that circuit is shut for the season in the story, and the chapter's travel takes Twinsen on.
- **The same shape every time:**
  1. **Arrival:** a few lines on landing.
  2. **Quest:** a short quest of our own on that island, from 5 to 15 minutes.
  3. **Gate:** the race opens once the quest is done.
  4. **Race:** beat that track's main opponent.
  5. **Clue:** what Twinsen learns about the masked driver.
  6. **Travel:** the way to the next island.
- **The original game's quests are ignored.** Original actors whose scripts would block a chapter, or pull it into LBA2's own plot, are
  silenced in story mode, as the build already does on Citadel and Celebration.
- **The masked driver** shows up from chapter 3 on: parked at the finish line, gone when Twinsen gets there.
- **The specials stay outside the story:** the Temple of Bú (Halloween) and The Long Table (Thanksgiving) remain seasonal side races in
  Specials. They're optional and never needed to finish the Cup. The Temple's line comes out of the story set.

### 0. Polar Island: the dream (built)

- **Race:** Twinsen dreams he is chasing FunFrock up Polar Island to Sendell. This is the chase as it stands, starting at the wheel.
- **New: one line at the finish.** Sendell's light says: *"Two worlds will race, Twinsen. He will be waiting at the end."*
- **Travel:** the white fade, then Zoe wakes him at home, as built.

### 1. Citadel 1: the storm (built, with a new quest)

- **Arrival (built):**
  - Zoe is sick of the rain and sends Twinsen to the weather wizard.
  - The wizard needs the lighthouse, but Raph won't leave the track.
  - Raph says: "beat my time".
  - Mr. Paul tells Twinsen: "driving gloves first!"
- **New quest: The stolen gloves.**
  - Zoe says Twinsen's driving gloves were in the washing line basket, and now they're gone.
  - Townsfolk saw a thief running towards the harbour.
  - Twinsen corners the thief behind the harbour warehouse. He hands the gloves back: *"Fine, fine. See you on the town circuit, if they
    ever build one."*
  - This introduces the thief as a town racer.
  - It replaces the gloves gate's current condition.
- **Race:** beat Raph's time (built).
- **Afterwards (built):**
  - Raph, the wizard and Zoe go to the lighthouse and the rain stops.
  - The Esmers land and thank Twinsen. New line: *"...and tomorrow we open a Cup for both worlds."*
  - Twinsen is tired and goes to bed.

### 2. Citadel 2: the town circuit

- **Arrival:**
  - Morning. The Esmers' town circuit is ready.
  - An Esmer at the start line explains the Two Worlds Cup: fourteen islands, one race each, and one wish for the winner.
  - Mr. Paul runs the race. His prize is the ferry ticket (built).
- **New quest: Zoe's car.**
  - Zoe wants to race too, but her car has no back wheel.
  - Twinsen finds a wheel in the harbour scrap and brings it to her.
  - Without it the gate says: *"Mr. Paul: six cars on the grid or no race."*
  - Zoe is already on the town roster.
- **Race:** beat Mr. Paul (built: 3 laps, ferry ticket).
- **Clue:** for a moment a black car stands at the finish line. Then it's gone. Raph says: *"Who was that?"*
- **Travel:** the ferry to the Desert.

### 3. Desert Island

- **Arrival:** the Desert champion (the racer) laughs at a ferry-ticket driver.
- **New quest: The Dean's lost pages.**
  - The Dean of the School of Magic has shut the track because the engines scattered his exam pages.
  - Three pages lie in places beside the road, so collecting them is also a tour of the track.
  - Bring all three back and the Dean opens the gate.
- **Race:** beat the racer.
  - Line-up: Moya, the Dino-Fly, the Dean, Baldino.
- **Clue:** Moya found oil on the last bend before the race. *"That wasn't there yesterday. A black car came through at night."*
- **Travel:**
  - Baldino, beaten, says his lander on the Emerald Moon is faster than any car.
  - He offers to take Twinsen up in his rocket.

### 4. Emerald Moon

- **Arrival:** the moon base's airlock is shut because HAL has cracked its screen and locked everything.
- **New quest: Mend HAL.**
  - The base's mechanics, the grey Franco guards with tools, are hiding from HAL.
  - Twinsen finds a spare screen in the stores and brings it, and the mechanics follow him back.
  - HAL is mended. Its working sprite comes back in place of the cracked one.
  - HAL insists on racing.
- **Race:** beat Baldino in his lander.
  - HAL drives itself.
- **Clue:** HAL's log shows a black car launched from the moon to Zeelich the night before.
- **Travel:** the Esmer ship takes the Cup on to Zeelich.

### 5. Otringal

- **Arrival:** Otringal's race marshal wants an entry fee in Zeelich money, and Twinsen has none.
- **New quest: The entry fee.**
  - Twinsen wins it in the casino using the slot machines (built for the race).
  - He needs three wins, or one jackpot.
  - Without the fee the gate says: *"The marshal: fee first, stranger."*
- **Race:** the Otringal race (beat the racer).
  - The prize wheel and the golden Gazogem are built.
- **Clue:**
  - A Dark Monk priest watches from the crowd.
  - A driver tells Twinsen that the souvenir seller on Celebration Island sells pictures of every car he's seen, including a black one.
- **Travel:** from the spaceport back to Twinsun, to Celebration Island.

### 6. Celebration 1: the lava lake (built: the seller's race)

- **Arrival:** the statue isn't up yet. The seller knows something but tells only a driver who beats him (built).
- **New quest: The seller's stall.**
  - The seller won't even race until his souvenirs are back on his stall.
  - The kangaroo knocked them all over the island, so Twinsen collects them.
- **Race:** beat the seller (built: rematch on a loss, information on a win).
- **Clue (the seller's information, rewritten):**
  - On Island CX he saw Franco engineers build a black car with no number.
  - It runs on refinery gazogem.
- **Travel:** the Esmer ship to the Island of the Francos.

### 7. Island of the Francos

- **Arrival:** De La Fontaine runs the refinery and the race, and he won't talk about who buys his fuel.
- **New quest: The fuel sample.**
  - Twinsen scoops a sample from the Desert's oil slick. He has carried it since chapter 3.
  - Mr. Kurtz tests it in his laboratory. It's the refinery's own gazogem.
  - Kurtz needs the nurse's help, so Twinsen finds her pram in the village first.
- **Race:** beat De La Fontaine in the gazogem tanker.
  - Line-up: Mr. Kurtz, the nurse.
- **Clue:**
  - De La Fontaine admits he sold a tanker of fuel to a masked buyer.
  - It was sent down the Elevator Platform to the undergas.
- **Travel:** to the Elevator Platform.

### 8. The Elevator Platform (head-to-head)

- **Arrival:** the monkey monster sits in his crate on the elevator and won't let anyone down without a race.
- **No quest.** It's straight into the race, as the user asked for this track (just Twinsen and the monster).
- **Race:** beat the monkey monster.
- **Clue:** the monster grumbles that a black car went down before Twinsen *"and didn't race me either."*
- **Travel:** the elevator goes down to the undergas.

### 9. Wannies Island

- **Arrival:** the Wannies' race is off because a mine cart is missing, and the miner won't race without his crew.
- **New quest: The lost mine cart.**
  - A Wannie and his mine cart are stuck down the tower.
  - Twinsen climbs down to them and pulls the lever that sends them back up.
  - The frog's mouth opens and shuts on a timer, so the way down is a bit of timing (built for the race).
- **Race:** beat the Wannie miner in his bulldozer.
  - Line-up: the mine cart, the old Wannie.
- **Clue:**
  - The old Wannie says the black car paid them to dig a tunnel to Mosquibees Island.
  - *"Paid in Zeelich coins with a Dark Monk's face on them."*
- **Travel:** through the Wannies' tunnel.

### 10. Mosquibees Island

- **Arrival:** the Queen races only drivers she has allowed onto her island.
- **New quest: The Queen's pass.**
  - The pass is in the hive.
  - The dissidents, the Queen's enemies, offer to help if Twinsen carries a message for them after the race.
- **Race:** beat the Queen.
  - Line-up: the monkey monster in his war cart.
- **Clue:** the dissidents' message is a map. The black car's base is under Celebration Island, in the volcano.
- **Travel:** the dissidents' ferry to Volcano Island.

### 11. Volcano Island

- **Arrival:** the dissidents' hide-out is under attack. Lava balls are falling.
- **New quest: The hide-out.**
  - Twinsen helps the hide-out's Mosquibee seal the cracks by bringing three slabs of lava rock.
  - The dissident then races, his way of thanking Twinsen.
- **Race:** beat the dissident.
  - Line-up: the ferryman, the hide-out's Mosquibee.
- **Clue:**
  - The dissident has seen the masked driver without his mask: FunFrock.
  - He builds his cars on Island CX and buys his soldiers from the Emperor's palace.
- **Travel:** the ferryman, beaten, takes Twinsen in his boat to Island CX.

### 12. Island CX

- **Arrival:** Island CX is a fortress, with cannons on the walls and the door to the halls shut.
- **New quest: Through the door.**
  - The survivor, the Franco on his raft, knows the door code but has forgotten half of it.
  - Twinsen finds the other half on the cannon crew's notice board.
- **Race:** beat the Emperor's soldier in his rocket launcher.
  - Line-up: the Franco trooper's tank, the survivor.
  - The race goes down through the door and round the halls (built).
- **Clue:**
  - The black car's workshop in the halls is empty.
  - A note on the bench says: *"To the palace. The Emperor will lend me his teleporter, or lose his throne."*
- **Travel:** the soldiers' transport to the Emperor's palace.

### 13. The Palace (built: the sprint out of the window)

- **Arrival:** FunFrock has taken the palace. The Emperor is trying to get out.
- **New quest: The Emperor's escape.**
  - Twinsen finds the Emperor hiding in the library.
  - Stan, the man with the time machine, says the only way out is the round window at the end of the long hall: *"and the Dark Monk's
    teleporter is just past it."*
- **Race:** the sprint to the window (built).
  - Beat Stan.
  - The road sweeper and the Emperor himself race too.
- **Clue:**
  - Won: the window shatters and the teleporter takes the car in mid-air (built).
  - The statue rises on Celebration Island (built: game variable 79).
- **Travel:** the teleporter, to Celebration.

### 14. Celebration 2: round the statue (the final)

- **Arrival:**
  - The Dark Monk's statue stands. FunFrock waits at the dock in the black car: *"We raced once, in your dream. This time it's real."*
  - **No quest.**
- **Race:** round the statue in a spiral to its shoulders, a ring round its head, and the bridge back down (built).
  - Beat FunFrock.
  - **New line-up:** FunFrock, the Dark Monk, the Sup priest and the Franco priest, whose cars are already made and are unlocked by this
    race today. The track currently uses the old racer, Baldino and the biker.
- **Ending:**
  - The Esmers hand Twinsen the Cup and ask for his wish.
  - Twinsen wishes for the statue to be gone, and with it FunFrock's way back.
  - Zoe, Raph and everyone from the Cup watch from the dock.
  - The Esmers' last line: *"Same time next year?"* That can lead into the seasonal specials.

## What the build needs (for later, not done)

**Built and reused as they are:**
- `gate=` (a quest's gate text)
- `win=`, `finish=`, `beat=` (a race sets the chapter on)
- `intro=`, `lose=` and the rematch
- `wake=` and `parade=`
- `unlock_win=` (each island's cars, as now)
- the text appending already used for the storm and the seller
- RaceTrackStory's scene-script edits (as on Citadel and Celebration)
- `StoryEntities` (keeping a character the race would remove)

**New:**
- **A chapter number:** a game variable for "which chapter", with each chapter's gate tied to it. This stops an island raced out of order.
  - Free variables: 209 and 210 are known to be free.
  - More will be needed: each quest needs one or two. They must be found with `scriptgrep2` before use.
- **Travel:** a story key for "after the win, say this, then change cube to the next island's arrival scene". The palace's teleporter
  and the ferry ticket already do this in their own way.
  - This replaces walking or sailing through LBA2's open world between chapters, which is how a player could find the original quests.
- **Shut circuits:** once won, a track's start line says *"Closed for the season"* in the story instead of starting a race.
- **Quest pieces:** the items to collect, which are pages, a wheel, souvenirs, lava slabs and a code. They are placed off the road the
  same way the ghosts and mushrooms are placed.
  - For icons, use LBA2's own inventory objects where one fits (a key, a letter, a tool), and an appended OBJFIX model otherwise, like
    the oil drum.
- **The masked driver:** a parked black car at each finish from chapter 2 to 12, hidden when Twinsen comes near.
  - This could be FunFrock's car, recoloured black.
- **Celebration 2's line-up:** FunFrock (main), the Dark Monk, the Sup priest and the Franco priest.
- **Silencing original quests:** go island by island, starting with the islands where an original actor talks over the new quest.

**Order of work:** finish chapters 1 and 2 first, since they're mostly built. Then work through the chapters in story order, one island
per round, each tested with the pilot from a save at the chapter's start.
