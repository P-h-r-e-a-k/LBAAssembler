# Little Big Racing Adventure: the walkthrough

The story mode played from start to finish: where Twinsen goes, what he picks up, who he talks to and what they say. It follows the design
in [LBRA_STORY_SCRIPT.md](LBRA_STORY_SCRIPT.md), which has the build notes; this is the same story told as a player's guide. Names in
*italics* are placeholders.

**How the story works:**
- **One race per island.** The story visits fifteen tracks in order, and each is raced once. Lose, and you race it again until you win;
  once won, a track is closed for the season: driving back over its start line starts no qualifying lap and no grid. The dream is the
  exception: it's run once, win or lose.
- **Over the line, a cut-scene.** Win, and the game takes the wheel: the car brakes to a stop just past the line, Twinsen gets out, and
  the congratulations (and the prize) play before he can move again. On the volcano, Island CX and the lava lake the car stays yours:
  walk up to whoever has the next word.
- **A small quest before each race.** Someone has shut the track, or wants something first. Solve it, and the marshal drops the flag.
- **Zoe** is with Twinsen from chapter 2 on. She follows him on foot, and talking to her (Action beside her) gives a hint for whatever
  he's stuck on. In a race she runs the pit box by the start line: drive slowly through it (under 20 km/h) for a pit stop, once a lap.
  It refills your item box with a fuel can and undoes a shrink or a skid.
- **Two players:** player 2 is Zoe. She never races in story mode. On foot she goes where she likes and can pick things up; in a race
  she's in the pit box, and the pit stop is hers to give with Action as Twinsen's car comes through.
- **Baldino's radio** rings when Twinsen arrives on each island from chapter 2 on. Answer it from the inventory. Baldino has tuned the
  car for that track (its best setup) and gives a tip.
- **The magic slate** gets a new page on each island: the map of the track there, with its start line, checkpoints and pit box. Open it
  from the inventory; left and right turn the pages. Two pages are hidden: see the secrets in chapters 1 and 2.

**The tracks, in order:** Polar Island (the dream), Citadel 1 (the storm), Citadel 2 (the town circuit), the White Leaf Desert, the
Emerald Moon, Otringal, Celebration 1 (the lava lake), the Island of the Francos, the Elevator Platform, Wannies Island, Mosquibees
Island, the Island under Celebration, Island CX, the Emperor's palace and Celebration 2 (round the statue).

## Chapter 0: The dream (Polar Island)

**Track:** Polar Island. **To beat:** FunFrock.

**1. The dock.** The game opens in a dream. Twinsen stands on the dock of a rocky island beside his car. FunFrock is already in his own
car, a big black one, revving the engine.

**FunFrock:** "You'll never catch me! Whoever makes it to the Sendells first will get all of their magical powers!"

FunFrock's car pulls away up the island.

**Twinsen:** "No, FunFrock, come back! What am I going to do? I need to stop him!"

A grobo comes up the dock to him.

**The grobo:** "Quick, Twinsen, take my vehicle! The fate of the world rests upon your shoulders!"

**Found:** the digger's key (the race's own item).

**2. The chase.** Twinsen jumps in and follows. FunFrock has a few seconds' start. The road climbs the island, round a loop and over
the jumps, to a ramp that leaps onto the rocky peak.

*Tip:* FunFrock takes the loop wide. Stay on the inside and use the digger key on the straight after it.

*Aside:* south-west of the dock, on Fortress Island's docks (LBA1's), the Dino-Fly stands at the end of the wooden jetty, where he lands
in LBA1. **The Dino-Fly:** "Where do you want to go?" (Brundle Island / Tippet Island) -- "This is not the game for that, Twinsen."

**3. The peak.** The race is run once. Whoever reaches the peak first, the dream ends there: as Twinsen's car leaves the lip of the
ramp (or as FunFrock's takes the jump ahead of him, Twinsen watching), everything turns white. Nobody speaks: the dream just ends.
(Sendell's prophecy, "Two worlds will race, Twinsen. And at the end of it, he will be waiting.", isn't said in the game: the race
file has no line for a dream's end.)

**4. Waking up.** Twinsen is in his bed at home. Zoe shakes him awake and kisses him.

**Zoe:** "Twinsen! Twinsen, wake up!"

If he caught FunFrock in the dream: **Zoe:** "You were smiling in your sleep. And steering."

If he didn't: **Zoe:** "You were shouting in your sleep. Something about a big man in a big car."

## Chapter 1: The storm (Citadel 1)

**Track:** the storm track round Citadel Island, in the rain. **To beat:** Raph's best lap.

**1. Home.** Zoe is by the window, looking at the rain.

**Zoe:** "Twinsen, I am sick of all this rain! Go and find the Weather Wizard and get him to fix it!"

**Zoe:** "Oh, and you left your driving gloves on the seat of your car, under the carport. This morning they were gone! Somebody's
been light-fingered again."

Talking to Zoe again: **Zoe:** "Tell me, Twinsen, have you found your gloves yet?" **Twinsen:** "Not yet. But I'll find whoever took
them."

**2. The Weather Wizard's tent.** Twinsen goes out of the house. Zoe comes out after him:

**Zoe:** "I'm off to watch the race practice. Don't forget that you can change the view by pressing "ENTER"."

She goes off up the path towards the storm track (she walks all the way out of the yard), and from then on she watches the practice from
beside the start line. Twinsen goes down the path and along the road to the Wizard's tent.

**The Weather Wizard:** "I was going to clear the rain, but I need to do it from the top of the lighthouse, and that rascal Raph is too
busy at the race track and refuses to let me."

The arrow points at the storm track's start line.

**3. The storm track.** The track runs where the Tavern used to stand: the Tavern was taken down to make room for it. Raph is lapping in
his car. When Twinsen steps onto the start line, Raph screeches to a stop beside him.

**Raph:** "Hey! Get off the track, Twinsen! Are you trying to get yourself run over?!"

(A townsman wanders along the track too. When Raph comes up behind him, he screeches to a stop and yells at him -- **Raph:** "Hey, you!
Get off the track! Are you trying to get yourself run over?!" -- then drives on.)

**Raph:** "I'm having too much fun, it can wait. I'll tell you what: if you can beat my time on the track, then I'll come with you to
the lighthouse. Mind you, you'll need a car and some driving gloves to take part."

Mr. Paul runs the start line.

**Mr. Paul:** "Sorry Twinsen, for safety reasons you'll need some driving gloves to take part."

Flick, the marshal, stands on her plinth beside the line with her flag.

**Flick:** "I'm the School of Magic's assistant, but on race days I'm the marshal. No gloves, no flag, I'm afraid. I saw a fellow with a
pair stuffed in his pocket go past where the Tavern used to be."

With his gloves back: **Flick:** "Gloves on, I see. Drive up to the line whenever you're ready, and I'll drop the flag for you."

Zoe stands a little way along the track from Raph's parking place, watching:

**Zoe:** "Raph's been lapping since breakfast, rain or no rain. Get your gloves back, Twinsen, and show him how it's done!"

With the gloves: **Zoe:** "Gloves on? Then go and beat his time! I'll be watching from right here."

**4. Where the Tavern stood.** All that's left of the Tavern is its doorstep. Tim the waiter stands on it in the rain, holding a tray.

**Tim:** "Twenty years I served drinks here, Twinsen, and now look: a car park. They needed the room for the track, they said."

**Tim:** "A fellow ran past with gloves in one hand and a customer's umbrella in the other. Towards the harbour! If you catch him, I want
that umbrella back. Not that I've anywhere to put it."

The arrow points at the harbour.

Talking to Tim again while the thief is still about (and once the umbrella is back):

**Tim:** "And don't ask me for a drink, Twinsen: the mayor's banned the stuff while there's a race track on the island. No drinking
and driving, he says. Funny, though... nobody's seen Luc all day. Nor the Tavern's bottles. Not my business. I'm only the waiter."

**5. The harbour.** The thief is lurking behind the warehouse. As Twinsen comes close:

**The thief:** "Can't catch me!"

He dodges round the warehouse and drops down the sewer hatch on the quay. The hatch is left open, and Twinsen climbs down after him.

**6. The sewers.** Down the ladder it's dark and dripping. Barrels line the walkways, the canals run green, and gargoyle pillars
guard the far tunnel. The thief is gone, but there are splashes somewhere ahead. A painter is at work on one of the signs.

**The painter:** "What a drag these sewers are! It stinks in here and I've still got a whole bunch of signs to update. Phew! I'm up to
my neck in it!!"

Talking to him again:

**The painter:** "A fellow in a hurry? He went through to the far tunnel, and trod on my wet paint. Follow the footprints."

Twinsen follows the painted footprints along the walkways, past the barrels, through the far tunnel and into the second sewer.

**7. The dead end.** The footprints stop under a grating, where the thief is trapped with nowhere left to run. Twinsen walks up to him
and presses Action.

**The thief:** "Fine, fine! Here are your gloves. Nice gloves, too. See you on the town circuit, if they ever build one."

**Found:** Driving gloves.

The thief drops the umbrella and scrambles up the ladder under the grating. Twinsen picks up the umbrella.

**Found:** Customer's umbrella.

The same ladder brings Twinsen up onto the quay.

**8. The umbrella (optional).** Back at the Tavern's doorstep, Twinsen gives Tim the umbrella.

**Tim:** "Our customer's umbrella! I'd stand you a drink on the house, if I still had a house. Here."

**Found:** 10 kashes.

**Secrets (optional, any time this chapter).** Two things on Citadel that pay off in chapter 2: each gives a page for the magic slate. (The racing overalls in the museum are not optional any more: Mr. Paul wants them on the storm track, 2026-11-08.)

- **The museum.** Twinsen takes the lift up the rampart and goes into the museum. The guard at the desk lets him in free (say yes to
  the ticket). Miss Bloop, the curator, is in the hall.

  **Miss Bloop:** "You showed up at the right time. The door at the top of the stairs is jammed, and your racing overalls are up
  there: Zoe lent them to us for the racing exhibition. Free the door, and you can have them back for the races."

  Once the door is free: **Miss Bloop:** "Thank you for freeing the door, Twinsen. Your racing overalls suit you: good luck at the
  races." **Found:** the racing overalls (item 4, the tunic's place: they store Magic as the tunic did). From now on Twinsen wears
  them. Mr. Paul lets nobody onto the storm track without them, nor without the driving gloves.

  On the hall's north wall, by the corner, over a sideboard, hangs a picture of Twinsen, head and shoulders, holding a gold cup. Action
  in front of it:

  *A portrait in a gold frame. Its plate reads: TWINSEN, HERO OF TWINSUN. The painter has put a winner's cup in his hands... a cup he
  has never won. Not yet.*

  A visitor walking the halls, spoken to: "Hey!... you know, you look like the guy in the picture! The one with the trophy!"

  In the hall, under the window, a display case like the one with the old book beside it: under its glass, a sheet of blueprints
  (Action by the case).

  *A file of blueprints: "POLAR ISLAND CIRCUIT. Construction plans. Not to be built." A road from the dock round the island's rocky head,
  a jump, a zigzag climb up the terraces, a high road along the cliffs, and a ramp leaping onto the peak.*

  **Twinsen:** "That's the track from my dream! Every bend of it. If only I had something to copy it onto."

  Talking to Miss Bloop about it:

  **Miss Bloop:** "That old thing? A man with glasses and a very big car left it here years ago. He said it was worth a fortune one
  day. It's never been worth anything to me, but I keep it, because I keep everything."

  Twinsen puts the file back under the case.

- **The neighbour.** Next door to Twinsen's house, the neighbour sits at his table with a sheet of paper and a magnifying glass.

  **Twinsen:** "Hello neighbour."

  **The neighbour:** "Somebody pushed this under my door the night they built the storm track. Drawings, I think, but the rain got to
  it and the ink's all faded. With a little gallic acid I could bring it back. There's none left on the island, mind: the pharmacy's
  waiting for its delivery."

**The secret bar (optional, any time this chapter -- an easter egg nobody points the way to).** With the Tavern gone and the mayor's
ban on drink, Luc and his regulars have vanished. By the Weather Wizard's tent, the mushroom man has noticed (once a visit, after his
line about the rain):

**The mushroom man:** "Have you seen Luc, from the Tavern? Nor has anyone, since the mayor banned drink. And half his regulars go
missing of an evening, and come home singing. Funny, that. The mushrooms hear everything."

They are in the Tralu's caves, the cave's mouth in the cliff below the tent: through its first hall (scene 2), the door at its far end
into the third hall (scene 20: the ladder up to the back walk stands in a pool, the 1996 demo's; walked into, its front row drowns
him, so a run and a jump (sporty) over it lands him in the shallows at the ladder's foot, and up the ladder (normal) to the back walk;
the lever on its platform to open the walk's gate, and the door at its end) and so into the second hall (scene 19), made bigger: the
Back Room. A massive old wooden table runs down the middle of it with a
bench along either side, plates, bowls, bottles and an oil lamp on it; old barrels stand round the edge as tall round tables, a bottle
and a plate on each; two iron candlesticks against the north wall, and between them Luc behind a counter of crates. Pat and Fab of
Raph's band sit on the far bench, the visitor from the Tavern and the drunk rabbibunny on the near one, and -- once he has his
customer's umbrella back -- Tim serves at the table. By the round table at the north wall, past the old room's arch, stands the Tralu
with a big tankard of beer on the barrel in front of him (he has left his own hall next door: it is empty now). Each talks when Twinsen
walks up to them and presses Action:

**Luc:** "Shh! Come in, Twinsen, and keep it down. Welcome to the Back Room: the only bar on Citadel the mayor doesn't know about. A
proper table, and the Tavern's last bottles... And if you're racing today, it's lemonade for you."

**Luc:** "And between us: regulars don't come the long way round. There's a back gate, up from the old hall next door, and a key to it
hidden where Sendell keeps an eye on it. You didn't hear that from me."

(Talked to again, Luc reminds him of the key until the gate is open, then only welcomes him.)

**The drunk rabbibunny:** "The mayor shays no drinking while there'sh a race track. [hic!] Sho I'm not drinking. I'm... sitting. At a
table. With a mug. [hic!] Don't tell the mayor."

**Pat:** "No Tavern, no stage... so we brought the band down here. Listen to that echo! Every beat comes back three times. Raph's
meant to be playing with us, but he won't come off that track."

**Fab:** "And the big fellow from next door came round to complain. He's still here. Third tankard."

**The visitor:** "Sorry, I'm not from here. Back home we drink in bars. Here you drink in a cave, in secret, in a storm, at a table
fit for a giant. I love this island."

**Tim** (from the umbrella given back until Twinsen is tired; his doorstep is empty meanwhile): "There you are, Twinsen! My shift on
the doorstep's over, so I'm on duty down here. Old habits: I can't sit in a bar without carrying a tray. Lemonade?"

The big fellow is the Tralu, from the hall next door (scene 2, past the first hall); he doesn't fight, he only grumbles:

**The Tralu:** "Grrrr... A hundred years in these caves, with nothing but the drips for company. Then the rain, then that race track,
and now the whole Tavern's moved into my back room, with drums! A Tralu can't hear himself growl. ...So I may as well have a tankard.
And stop offering me peanuts."

**The back gate.** From the bar, east past the table, steps go up through the stalagmites onto a landing, and from it a corridor runs
south over the water to a door into the Tralu's old hall: Twinsen comes out on a ledge, a barred gate behind him. Stairs go down from
the ledge's side along the hall's back wall, as wide as the ledge's side, to a landing, and turn there down into the hall. On the
ledge's face is a sign of Sendell (Action: "Aha! a sign from Sendell. They are venerated by all the
wizards."), and once Luc has told him, Action there turns up the regulars' key (a key jumps out). With it, back up the stairs, the barred
gate opens to him (the game's key door: the key is used, and the gate stays open for good), and the bar's quick way is open: the cave's
mouth, its first hall, the Tralu's hall, the stairs and the gate.

**9. The race.** Back at the start line, Raph has his time ready.

**Raph:** "A car and driving gloves: you're all set, Twinsen! My best lap is ## seconds. Beat that, and I'll come with you to the
lighthouse."

Flick drops the flag. It's one lap against Raph's time, with the rain on the road.

*Tip:* the track is slick. Brake before the bends, not in them, and keep off the puddles at the Tavern corner.

**10. Raph's time beaten.** Twinsen stops and gets out of his car. Raph has climbed out of his and walked over to Zoe; his car is
nowhere to be seen. Twinsen goes over and talks to him.

**Raph:** "You beat my time, Twinsen! A deal is a deal: I'm off to the lighthouse. See you there!"

He runs off down the road.

**Zoe:** "Twinsen, you did it! Raph's on his way to the lighthouse, and the Weather Wizard is meeting us there. Come on, let's go
together!"

She takes his arm, and they walk to the lighthouse together, slowly: down the path into the yard by the house, west over the beach, up
onto the lighthouse's hill and round its back to the door, where Raph and the Wizard are waiting.

**Flick**, asked after the race: "Raph's lap record, beaten in the pouring rain! I'll chalk your time on my plinth, before the rain
washes it off."

**11. The lighthouse.** Twinsen to the Wizard: "We are ready, Master." **The Wizard:** "Follow me!" They climb to the top. The Wizard
casts his spell. The thunder stops, and the rain thins to a drizzle.

**The Weather Wizard:** "There. A storm this old doesn't clear in an evening, mind you: give it the night. By morning you'll see the sun."

**Raph:** "Back to my car, then, and off to bed. Best race I've lost in years."

**Zoe:** "Something's up there, Twinsen. Above the clouds. Lights. Did you see them?"

Twinsen and Zoe are left on the top together. Down below, Raph sets off down the hill towards the race track and his car.

**The Weather Wizard:** "And now, home to my tent. Mind the puddles on your way down, Twinsen!"

He raises his staff, and he's gone in a flash of lightning.

The telescope on the balcony still works (the storm night through it: see the easter eggs). Going down -- Action facing the lamp room's
door -- or talking to Zoe:

**Zoe:** "Are you coming home, Twinsen?"
- **"Yes, let's go home."** She takes his arm again, and they go down and walk home together.
- **"I'll come home soon."** **Zoe:** "Don't stay up here too long, then. I'll light the candle by the bed for you." She goes down
  ahead of him, and Twinsen goes down when he likes.

**12. Home to bed.** On the way home, everyone Twinsen talks to says the same:

"You look tired, Twinsen. You should go home and have a nap."

If he passes the Tavern's doorstep: **Tim:** "Lights over the sea, at this hour? First a race track, now this. I'm going home." And he
goes, off along the road and out of sight; he isn't on the doorstep again that night.

At home the house is dark but for the candle on the bedside table. Zoe is there: in with Twinsen, or waiting by the dining table.

**Zoe:** "Bed, Twinsen. The Wizard said by morning, and morning comes quicker when you're asleep."

Upstairs, Twinsen goes to his bed (from either side, or its foot).

**Twinsen:** "What a day! A good night's sleep..."

He gets into bed.

**Zoe:** "Goodnight, Twinsen. Don't forget the candle."

**Twinsen:** "Goodnight, Zoe."

He blows out the candle. Darkness.

## Chapter 2: The town circuit (Citadel 2)

**Track:** the Esmers' town circuit round Citadel. **To beat:** Mr. Paul. Six cars, three laps.

**1. The morning.** Sunlight through the window. Zoe shakes Twinsen awake again, and this time she's excited.

**Zoe:** "Twinsen! Wake up! The aliens have landed! A big ship, right by the harbour road, and the whole island's out to look!"

**Twinsen:** "Aliens? I was dreaming about... no. Never mind. I'm coming!"

Twinsen goes out of the front door. The island has changed overnight: the sky is clear, the storm track is gone, a new circuit runs
round the whole town, and the Tavern is standing again where it always stood.

**2. The landing.** The Esmers' ship stands beside the new circuit, with islanders crowding round it. As Twinsen comes near, an Esmer
steps forward.

**The Esmer:** "People from the planet Twinsun, we come to you in a spirit of peace. Thank you for clearing the rain which was preventing
us from landing."

**The Esmer:** "We saw your little race track in the rain. Brave, but small, and it cost you your Tavern. So last night we built you a
bigger one, round your whole town, and we put your Tavern back where it stood, every stone of it."

**The Esmer:** "And today we open a Cup for both of our worlds, Twinsun's and Zeelich's. The first race is here, on your new circuit."

The islanders round the ship each have something to say:

**A Quetch:** "A whole circuit in one night! I went to bed with a puddle outside my door and woke up with a grandstand."

**A Grobo:** "Did you see the Tavern? Back like it never left. Even the sign's been repainted."

**A rabbibunny:** "They asked me where the bins went. Polite, these aliens."

**3. The Tavern.** Raph stands outside the Tavern, staring at it.

**Raph:** "I parked on this spot last night. Now there's a Tavern on it. Where's my car?"

**Raph:** "...in the Esmers' car park, they say. With a wash and a polish. I could get used to aliens."

Tim is at the door, then behind his bar inside. Twinsen goes in.

**Tim:** "Twinsen! Look at it! The same bar, the same stools, the same stain on the ceiling... I asked them to leave that out, but they
said 'exactly as it was'. Come in, come in: the first drink in the new old Tavern is on the house."

If Twinsen gave the umbrella back: **Tim:** "And that umbrella you brought back is on its hook by the door. She never came for it.
Take it, if you like: you've earned it, and she'd only lose it again."

If he didn't: **Tim:** "And if you ever find my regular's umbrella, there's a hook by the door waiting for it."

While Luc's car is still a wheel short: **Tim:** "Luc's not in yet: he's out in the yard by your house with that barrel car of his, a
wheel short."

The umbrella hangs on its hook just inside the front door. Action by it:

**Twinsen:** "Her umbrella. After yesterday, I'm not going anywhere without one."

**Found:** the umbrella. (Once Luc's car has its wheel, Luc is back behind his bar too.)

(The regulars are back in the Tavern, the Back Room's table is empty, and the Tralu, still at his round table with his tankard, is
glad of the quiet: **The Tralu:** "Ahhh... quiet at last. The aliens put the Tavern back, and the drums went home with it. The whole
bar to myself, and my tankard. Mind you, now they want me in their race. A Tralu in a car! Whatever next?")

**4. The yard.** In the yard north of Twinsen's house, where the cars park, Luc, the Tavern's boss, stands beside his car: a beer
barrel on wheels, with one back wheel missing. Zoe is already lying under it with a spanner.

**Luc:** "They gave me my Tavern back, Twinsen, so I'm racing for the Tavern! Only... my back wheel came off on the way here."

**Zoe:** "I can fit one, if someone finds a wheel. Felix at the harbour fished an old tyre out of the sea this morning."

The arrow points at the harbour. (Luc and Zoe stand right outside the house's door; they tell it once.)

If Twinsen goes home first, Zoe has left a word at the door: **Zoe:** "There's a crowd at the new track already! Come on, I want a
look... and bring your toolbox. Somebody's going to need a mechanic today."

**5. The start line.** The Esmer herald stands beside the line.

**The Esmer herald:** "Welcome to the Two Worlds Cup! One race on every island of Twinsun and Zeelich, and to whoever wins them all, a
wish. Any wish. The first race is here, on Citadel."

**The Esmer herald:** "Every racer gets one of these. It shows the circuits of the Cup: a new one appears each time you reach its
island."

**Found:** Magic Slate. Its first page is the map of the town circuit.

Flick is on her plinth again.

**Flick:** "Six cars on the grid or Mr. Paul won't start it. That's you, Raph, Mr. Paul, the Tralu, that thief again... and Luc, if he
ever gets his barrel back on four wheels."

**Mr. Paul:** "Rules are rules, Twinsen. Six cars."

**6. The bait.** Mr. Bazoo's shop sells fishing bait (any time): a tin on the counter. Twinsen faces it and presses Action.

**Mr. Bazoo:** "A tin of fishing bait costs 2 kashes." "OK, I'll buy it!"

**Twinsen:** "Fishing bait. Somebody at the harbour will be glad of this."

**7. The harbour.** Felix is by the scrap heap on the quay, an old tyre beside him.

**Felix:** "See this? An old tyre I fished out of the harbour this morning, rim and all. Luc's been asking everyone for a wheel. It's
yours for a tin of bait: the fish won't bite on rust. Mr. Bazoo sells it in his shop."

With the bait, Twinsen hands it over at once:

**Twinsen:** "Here: a tin of bait, fresh from Mr. Bazoo's."

**Felix:** "Deal! The tyre's yours. Mind, there might still be a crab in it."

**Twinsen:** "A tyre. A bit rusty, but round."

(The bait, the tyre and Luc can come in any order: with the tyre before Luc has told, **Luc:** "A tyre! Twinsen, you read my mind: my
back wheel came off on the way here, and I'm racing for the Tavern!")

**8. Back to the yard.** Zoe fits the tyre in no time.

**Zoe:** "There. Good as new. Well, good as old."

**Luc:** "Ha! First round's on me, both of you. After the race."

**Zoe:** "And Twinsen, a parcel came for you on the morning ferry, from Baldino on the Desert. A portable radio! He says he wants to hear
how the Cup goes."

**Found:** Portable Radio.

**Zoe:** "You drive, I'll keep the car in one piece. I'll be in the pit box by the start line."

**9. The first call.** The radio beeps. The first time, a line on screen says: "The radio is ringing. Answer it from your inventory."
Twinsen opens the inventory and uses the radio.

**Baldino** (radio): "Hello? Twinsen? Can you hear me? Excellent, the radio works! Listen, I've been reading about this Cup. I tuned your
car before you ever knew it needed tuning: shorter gears for the town's tight corners. Try not to scratch the paint."

(If the radio isn't answered, it answers itself after twenty seconds.)

**10. The race.** Twinsen drives to the start line: out of the yard north, onto the circuit and round its east side to the grid behind
the line. First a qualifying lap: over the line. Stopped at the line, the car is put on pole: "Qualifying lap: over the start line!"
(Before the wheel is on: "Mr. Paul: six cars! Luc's a wheel short.") Then six cars on the grid: Twinsen, Mr. Paul,
Raph, the Tralu, the thief and Luc's barrel. Flick drops the flag. Three laps; beat Mr. Paul. Zoe waves from the pit box on each lap.

*Tip:* Mr. Paul drives a tugboat on wheels: slow off the line, hard to pass on the narrow streets. Get past him before the harbour
straight.

**Zoe** (at a pit stop): "Fuel's in, wheels are on. Go!"

**11. Won.** The car brakes to a stop just past the line and Twinsen gets out; Mr. Paul comes over with the prize.

**The trooper:** "Lost it? The survivor on the raft has it. Won't give it back, either. Says his motor's out of fuel."

**4. The survivor.** He's sitting on his raft at the shore.

**The survivor:** "The Key for the passage? Mine. Fair and square. But if you had some fuel for my raft..."

Twinsen gives him the Can of GazoGem from the Francos' island.

**The survivor:** "Gazogem! The good stuff! Here, take the key."

**Zoe:** "So much for evidence."

**Found:** Key for the passage to CX.

(If Twinsen has lost track of the can: **Zoe:** "The gazogem we scooped up on the Francos' dock. It's still in the toolbox... I mean,
your pocket.")

**5. The race.** Round the island under the cannons, down through the door and round the halls. The Emperor's soldier (the one to
beat), the Franco trooper's tank and the survivor on his raft.

*Tip:* the cannons fire where you're going to be. Change speed when you hear them, not direction.

**6. Won.**

**Lyra:** "Island CX is yours!"

In the empty workshop in the halls, a note lies on the bench (the arrow).

**The note:** "To the palace. The Emperor will lend me his teleporter, or he will lose his throne. F."

**Lyra:** "The palace! The soldiers' transport leaves now, and we're on it."

## Chapter 13: The Emperor's palace

**Track:** a sprint through the palace's halls and out of the round window. **To beat:** Stan.

**1. Arrival.** In the south-east room, Lyra stands before a sealed gate.

**Lyra:** "FunFrock has taken the palace! The gate's sealed with the Emperor's seal..."

Twinsen holds up the four Fragments: the Sups', the Francos', the Wannies' and the Mosquibees'. The gate opens.

**Lyra:** "...and it knows the Fragments of the four peoples when it sees them."

A new page on the slate: the palace.

**2. The radio rings.**

**Baldino** (radio): "Indoors! Marble floors, so soft tyres. And Twinsen, don't break anything, those vases are older than me."

**3. The Empress.** She is in the music room. Pighead guards patrol the halls on the way.

**The Empress:** "My husband! FunFrock's men have locked him in his bedroom. The lock answers to a song, but I can't sing."

At the Emperor's bedroom door, Twinsen sings the Ferryman's Song. It's... not good. The door opens anyway.

**Zoe:** "I've heard worse. Not much worse."

**4. The Emperor.**

**The Emperor:** "Twinsen of Twinsun! FunFrock has my teleporter, past the round window at the end of the long hall. If we get there
first, it's ours. Take this: you've earned it."

**Found:** Emperor's Sword.

**5. Stan.** He's at his desk in the library.

**Stan:** "The window? End of the long hall. I've timed that run from this desk more times than I've read these books. Race me there."

**6. The race.** The sprint to the window: Stan (the one to beat), the road sweeper and the Emperor himself.

*Tip:* the marble is slippery on the corners between rooms. Lift off before each doorway.

**7. Won.** Twinsen's car smashes through the round window, and the teleporter takes it in mid-air. A voice comes over the flight:

**FunFrock** (unseen): "Very good, Twinsen. Now come and finish it. I'll be waiting, just like in your dream."

On Celebration Island, the Dark Monk's statue rises.

## Chapter 14: Round the statue (Celebration 2): the final

**Track:** a spiral up the statue to its shoulders, a ring round its head and the bridge back down. **To beat:** FunFrock.

**1. The dock.** The teleporter sets Twinsen down on Celebration's dock. FunFrock stands beside his black car, no mask now.

If Twinsen caught him in the dream:

**FunFrock:** "We raced once, Twinsen, in your dream. You caught me, in your dream. This time it's real, and the wish is mine."

If he didn't:

**FunFrock:** "We raced once, Twinsen, in your dream. You never caught me then, and you won't now. This time it's real, and the wish
is mine."

The last page of the slate: round the statue.

**2. The last call.**

**Baldino** (radio): "Twinsen. Everything I have is on that car. Best setup, and one last thing in the boot: a super jet-pack. Use it on
the spiral. And Twinsen... win."

**3. The crowd.** Everyone has come to watch: Zoe, Raph, Mr. Paul, Baldino and Flick are on the dock.

**Zoe:** "Go on, Twinsen! For Citadel!"

**Raph:** "Beat his time like you beat mine!"

**Flick:** "Lyra's flag, my cheering."

**Mr. Paul:** "Six cars? Five will do, today. Rules are rules, but this is the final."

**4. The race.** FunFrock (the one to beat), the Dark Monk, a Sup priest and the Franco priest. Lyra drops the flag.

*Tip:* save the super jet-pack for the spiral, as Baldino said. FunFrock is slowest on the bridge back down.

If Twinsen loses: **FunFrock:** "Again? I have all day." The race starts again.

**5. Won.**

**Lyra:** "Twinsen of Citadel Island wins the Two Worlds Cup!"

**FunFrock:** "No! That wish was mine!"

An Esmer steps forward with the Cup.

**The Esmer:** "The Cup is yours, and its wish. What will it be?"

Twinsen chooses:

- **"The statue gone, and FunFrock with it."**

  **The Esmer:** "Granted."

  The statue crumbles away, and Celebration Island is as it was. FunFrock is gone with it.

- **"Another race. Right now."**

  **FunFrock:** "Ha! You'll regret that."

  The final is raced again, and the Esmer asks once more. Choosing another race a second time ends the game on FunFrock's laugh.

**6. The dock, after.**

**Zoe:** "Home, Twinsen?"

**Raph:** "Only if you let me win sometimes."

**Baldino:** "I'm keeping the car. For science."

**The Esmer:** "Same time next year? We hear there's a temple, and a very long table..."

The credits roll.

## The magic slate's pages

One page per island, added on arrival, from chapter 2: the town circuit, the White Leaf Desert, the Emerald Moon, Otringal, the lava
lake, the Francos' island, the Elevator Platform, Wannies Island, Mosquibees Island, the volcano, Island CX, the palace and the statue.

The dream and the storm track come before the slate, so their pages are hidden and optional, both on Citadel in chapter 2: Polar
Island from the museum's blueprints, and Citadel 1 from the neighbour's design sheet (with gallic acid from the pharmacy). They go in at
the front of the book, so a full slate has all fifteen tracks in story order.
