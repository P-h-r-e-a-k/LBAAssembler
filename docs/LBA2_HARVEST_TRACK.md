# The Long Table: the Thanksgiving race

A brand-new island and track for LBRA, made from nothing: no retail island, no LBA1 scenes. Files: `HARVEST.ILE`, `HARVEST.OBL`, scenes from 350.
Route and ground: `tools/RaceTrackPlan/harvest_design.py`. Build code: `Terrain/Harvest/`.

The user's brief, 2026-11-02: "Spend a couple of hours designing a thanksgiving track. This will be a totally new track we create from scratch
instead of based on anything. Let's make it Canadian thanksgiving related too and not just American".

## The idea

**One island, two Thanksgivings, one table.**

The two holidays are six weeks apart and share a menu, so the island is split by a border: the north half is Canada in October, the south half the
United States in November. Across the border lies the Long Table, a harvest table so large the race drives on it. The lap is a figure of eight:
**over the table from October into November, and under it, between its legs, back again.** You see the same table twice, from above and from below,
and the two crossings of the border are the same moment seen from each side.

The season moves with the car: red maples and falling leaves in the north; bare oaks, frost on the stubble and the first flurries in the south.

## What the research said

Canada:

- Second Monday of October, set by Parliament in 1957; a harvest thanks. Frobisher's 1578 service is credited as the first European thanksgiving in North
  America, and Champlain's Order of Good Cheer feasted at Port Royal in 1606-07 against the scurvy; Indigenous harvest thanks long predate both.
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving_(Canada)), [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/thanksgiving-day),
  [ICT](https://ictnews.org/archive/canadian-thanksgiving-from-age-old-harvest-festival-to-columbus-day-coincidence/)
- Cranberry bogs are harvested late September to mid-October; Bala in Muskoka calls itself the Cranberry Capital of Ontario, and the flooded bog with workers
  in hip waders is the sight of the season. [Statistics Canada](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/o1/en/plus/4708-fall-harvest-whats-season),
  [Destination Ontario](https://www.destinationontario.com/en-ca/articles/cranberry-festivals-and-flavours-ontario)
- Windsor, Nova Scotia's Pumpkin Regatta: people paddle hollowed-out giant Atlantic pumpkins across Lake Pesaquid, a half-mile course, an October
  tradition tied to the holiday, founded by Danny Dill, whose father Howard bred the Atlantic Giant. Its reports call it the longest-running pumpkin boat race.
  It was reported cancelled indefinitely in 2022 for lack of water; I found no word on whether it has returned, so the track honours it without naming it.
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsor_Pumpkin_Regatta), [Guinness](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/84303-longest-running-pumpkin-boat-race)
- The CFL's Thanksgiving Day Classic is a Monday doubleheader; the Kitchener-Waterloo Oktoberfest Thanksgiving Day Parade is called Canada's largest.
  [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thanksgiving_Day_Classic), [Kitchener-Waterloo Oktoberfest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kitchener%E2%80%93Waterloo_Oktoberfest)
- Canadian football: a field 110 yards long and 65 wide against 100 by 53.3, three downs against four, twelve players against eleven, and the posts on the goal
  line rather than at the back of the end zone (that last rests on fewer sources; the search found no sign of a 2026 change, nor proof there wasn't one).
  [REFR](https://refrsports.com/blog/cfl-vs-nfl-rules-key-differences-explained), [FootballScoop](https://footballscoop.com/rule-differences-between-the-cfl-and-nfl)
- The butter tart is Canadian, linked in accounts to early Quebec settlement and pioneer kitchens; Ontario has a butter tart trail since 2006. Nanaimo bars and
  tourtière are the other Canadian table names. [Reader's Digest Canada](https://www.readersdigest.ca/?p=280621), [Canadian Food Focus](https://canadianfoodfocus.org/food/raisins-or-no-raisins-canadas-superstar-tart/)

The United States:

- Fourth Thursday of November; the 1621 Plymouth harvest. Parade: 1924, giant balloons from 1927. Football: Detroit has played on the day since 1934. The turkey
  pardon. [DOGOnews](https://services.dogonews.com/2019/11/19/the-origins-of-five-popular-thanksgiving-traditions), [Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/science/pardon-a-turkey-7-thanksgiving-traditions-explained.print)
- The shared table: turkey, stuffing, mash, gravy, cranberry, pumpkin pie. [Diffen](https://www.diffen.com/difference/American_Thanksgiving_vs_Canadian_Thanksgiving)

## What the race won't do

For many Native people Thanksgiving recalls colonisation: since 1970 the United American Indians of New England have marked it as a National Day of Mourning, and
others celebrate; Indigenous peoples aren't one opinion, and the warning from Native writers is against the romanticised footnote.
[San José Public Library](https://sjpl.org/blog/native-perspectives-thanksgiving-native-american-heritage-month), [VOA](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/amp/thanksgiving-national-day-of-mourning/5182118.html),
[Culture Trip](https://theculturetrip.com/north-america/usa/new-york/articles/the-native-american-experience-of-thanksgiving-sorrow-apathy-joy)

So: **no Pilgrims-and-Indians tableau, no costumed Indigenous figures, no headdresses, nobody as a mascot or a hazard.** The track is about harvest, food, weather and
parades. History appears as places, not people: the fort, the ships. A planned Three Sisters garden beside the maze, corn with beans and squash, would credit the
Haudenosaunee who gave it that name: a real farming system rather than a costume.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Sisters_(agriculture)), [Onondaga Nation](https://www.onondaganation.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/three_sisters_book.pdf)

No team, league or Macy's name or logo: generic floats and teams only.

## The lap

A clockwise loop of Canada and an anticlockwise loop of the States, crossing at the table. About 1,900 cells, the size of the Temple of Bú's (1,833).

| # | Section | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Fall Fair | The start on the north straight, heading east, pit lane on the right. Tents, Ferris wheel, hay bales, prize pumpkins. |
| 2 | The Regatta | On over the lake of the giant pumpkin boats, then a leap over their lane, landing as high as it leaves. |
| 3 | The Maple Run | Bends up through the red hills, leaves falling, rising to table height. |
| 4 | The Horn of Plenty | Up the cloth into the cornucopia's tip and out of its mouth onto the table. First border crossing is on the cloth. |
| 5 | The Long Table | West along the gingham. Canada's end: butter tarts, pumpkin pie. The States' end: pecan pie, sweet potato. Round the turkey and Mash Mountain, gravy erupting. |
| 6 | The Pepper Mill | Off the table's end and down round the mill, a turn and a quarter. |
| 7 | The Harbour | South along the wharves, the drawbridge's leap over the inlet, the tall ships. |
| 8 | Parade Avenue | East along the south coast, fast, under the giant balloons, past the reviewing square. |
| 9 | The Turkey Bowl | Through the stadium and round the field, footballs coming down. |
| 10 | The Corn Maze | Narrow, walled in corn, two hairpins. |
| 11 | Under the Table | North between the table's legs and the chairs', a chicane, the dark rug overhead. Second border crossing, November back into October. |
| 12 | The Bog | West on a boardwalk over the flooded red beds, geese coming in to land. |
| 13 | The Habitation | Through the courtyard of the fort, the Order of Good Cheer's feast laid in its hall. Round onto the north straight. |

The stadium is **both countries'**: one end of the field has goalposts on the goal line and the other at the back of the end zone, and the field is marked to the longer length.

## The hazards

Reskins of systems the engine already has, so they behave as players already expect:

| Hazard | Theme | Engine |
|---|---|---|
| Gravy eruptions on the table | Mash Mountain's crater, every 6 s or so | `lavaGeysers`, a gravy ball's model |
| Footballs coming down | Kicked from mid-field onto the road round it, their shadows growing | `crystalShards`, a football's model |
| Canada geese | Two flocks of seven in Vs, overhead | `spook` |
| Parade balloons | A turkey, a pumpkin and a goose over the avenue | `spook` |
| Pumpkin boats | Four, paddling the lane under the leap | `spook` at water level |
| A bunch of balloons (the power-up) | One sent to each rival | `haunt_balloons`, as the Desert's |
| Item boxes | Mini pumpkins | The Temple's |

## The look

- Palette: Otringal's with thirty harvest colours written into its nearest slots (`HarvestPalette`), as the Temple's is; the cars and display stay right. Amber fog.
- Ground: every tile drawn by code (`HarvestTiles`), nothing borrowed.
- Sky: late-afternoon gold over a slate lake.
- Falling leaves while Twinsen is north of the border and snow south of it: the engine's snowfall in the palette's maple colours (`leaves=`).
- The road changes with the place: a cranberry runner edged in gold down the table, boardwalk over the bog, cinders round the stadium.
- Music and birdsong: Citadel Island's.

## Limits it was designed to

| Rule | The Long Table |
|---|---|
| No downward jumps | Both leaps land exactly as high as they leave |
| Jumps in one cube, lips off cube edges | Checked by the script |
| Steepest driven grade | 13.7 % |
| Tightest bend | Radius 8 cells, in the maze |
| Road over road | 3,820 units at the least |
| Body limit 550 points | Table cut into 40-cell pieces |
| Big faces lose depth | Table top in 4-cell squares |
| One island byte, one palette, one sky | Island byte 4, RESS 41 and 25, both empty in retail |
| Frame rate | 49.8 fps through the fair, the lake and the table at 848x480, worst frame 43 ms |

## Status

Built and driven by the autodriver, not yet by a person:

- The island, ground, sky, palette and about 1,050 placed things, every model made from nothing (`HarvestBodies`).
- Time trial: two clean laps of about 61 s, no missed checkpoint, no rescue.
- Race against Tim, the old Wannie and Bob: they lap in 76 to 82 s; the geyser, footballs and power-ups all fired.

Left out of this first version, for later:

- A goose that walks across the road, a turkey or goose car of the track's own, a slick in cranberry and gravy colours. The line-up uses existing cars that suit a feast.
- The Three Sisters garden isn't marked as such yet: the field by the maze is corn. Its credit should stay in this document until the garden is really there.
- Pit lane not driven in: no car has been tested going into it.
